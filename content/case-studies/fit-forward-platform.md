## The challenge

GrindKaro needed a member app beyond marketing pages — digital books, readable PDFs, purchases, and a cohesive brand experience across admin and consumer surfaces.

## Our approach

- **Vite + React** for fast iteration and lean bundles on Vercel
- Shared content model with **GrindKaro Admin** for ops
- PDF reader and catalog UX optimized for mobile-first athletes
- SEO and OG aligned with `grindkaro.in` brand

## The solution

`app.grindkaro.in` is the member hub — content consumption, owned products, and flows that connect to admin tooling and coaching funnels.

## Ecosystem

| Product         | Role                     |
| --------------- | ------------------------ |
| GrindKaro App   | Members & content        |
| GrindKaro Admin | Ops & purchases          |
| lift-stronger   | Subscriptions & coaching |

## Impact

- Live production domain serving the fitness coaching brand
- Unified ecosystem instead of one-off landing pages
- Platform for digital products alongside physical coaching
