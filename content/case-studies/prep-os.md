## The challenge

Interview prep tools are either too generic (leetcode clones) or too fragmented (Notion + timers + bookmarks). We needed one desktop-first experience for placement season — with SEO content that brings organic traffic.

## Our approach

- **Next.js App Router** for marketing, blogs, and fast LCP on `prep-os.queztlabs.tech`
- **Electron shell** for focus lock, capture workflows, and offline-friendly study blocks
- **Metadata-first SEO** — titles, OG, Twitter cards, and structured content paths
- Iterative shipping on Vercel with preview branches per feature

## The solution

Prep OS ships as a public web presence plus a downloadable desktop app. Students get a 7-day plan, AI-assisted capture from problem statements, and lockdown mode during sprints.

| Layer     | Tech                    |

- Production domain with indexable blog/content surfaces
